# Aspire Survey

**S2M Health × Aspire — Workplace Behaviour & Impact Survey 2026**

A multi-step employee survey for active S2M Health employees participating in the Aspire habit formation program. The survey measures behaviour change, capability development, manager support, habit internalisation, real workplace impact evidence, and barriers since April 2026.

## Who it's for

Active S2M Health employees enrolled in Aspire, across 11 roles (SME / Process Coach, HCC Coders, HR, Team Leads, Admin, Quality Analyst, Trainer, IT Manager, IT, MIS, Team Manager).

## What it does

- Collects 22 structured questions across 13 sections
- Routes respondents through role-specific questions (Section 12) based on their Aspire Team/Role
- Conditional follow-up for Aspire attribution in the Evidence section
- Stores responses with Employee ID as the linkback key for joining with PMS, habit formation, attendance, productivity and quality data
- One submission per Employee ID (duplicate protection)
